📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

Transforming Workforce Data into Actionable HR Insights

📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project designed to analyze employee information, monitor key HR performance indicators, and identify workforce trends.

The dashboard helps HR professionals and business leaders understand employee demographics, analyze employee attrition, evaluate workforce distribution, and make informed decisions about employee retention and organizational performance.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze employee headcount and workforce demographics.
- Monitor employee attrition and retention trends.
- Examine department-wise workforce distribution.
- Analyze employee satisfaction and workplace trends.
- Identify workforce patterns affecting employee retention.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making.

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

KPI - Description 

Total Employees - Total number of employees in the dataset 
Attrition Count - Number of employees who left the organization 
Attrition Rate - Percentage of employees who left 
Active Employees - Number of employees currently working 
Average Age - Average age of employees 
Job Satisfaction - Employee satisfaction levels 
Department Distribution - Workforce distribution across departments 

🔍 Key Analysis Areas

1. Employee Demographics

- Employee distribution by age and gender.
- Workforce composition across departments.
- Employee distribution by education and job role.

2. Employee Attrition Analysis

- Overall employee attrition rate.
- Attrition patterns across departments and job roles.
- Attrition trends by age group and employee characteristics.

3. Department-Wise Workforce Analysis

- Department-level employee headcount.
- Workforce distribution by job designation.
- Comparison of attrition across departments.

4. Employee Satisfaction Analysis

- Job satisfaction levels across employees.
- Relationship between satisfaction and attrition.
- Employee satisfaction trends across job roles and departments.

5. Workforce Performance Indicators

- Workforce composition and employment trends.
- Employee tenure and experience patterns.
- Identification of areas requiring further HR investigation.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI: Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation.
- DAX: Calculated measures and KPI development.
- Excel/CSV: Dataset storage and preliminary analysis, where applicable.

💼 Business Value

The dashboard provides HR teams and management with insights to:

- Improve Employee Retention: Identify employee groups with higher attrition.
- Support Workforce Planning: Understand department-level staffing patterns.
- Monitor HR Performance: Track important workforce indicators.
- Understand Employee Satisfaction: Explore potential factors influencing employee experiences.
- Enable Data-Driven Decisions: Translate employee data into meaningful business insights.


🚀 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how HR data can be transformed into meaningful visual insights to support workforce analysis and strategic HR decision-making.

It highlights practical skills in data preparation, KPI development, data visualization, and business analytics.
